## 3. Proposed restrictions

Except where applicable law, an applicable third-party license, or written permission allows otherwise, users would not redistribute, resell, sublicense, or publicly upload the covered proprietary build, distribute modified copies, or remove ownership notices. These restrictions would apply only to material validly covered by the proprietary agreement. They do not restrict GPL redistribution, modification, source access, or any other rights granted by existing licenses.

Early Access would provide only the benefits accurately described at checkout. Preview builds may contain defects and may change before public release. Specific release dates, game outcomes, uninterrupted service, or future features are not guaranteed unless expressly promised.

Separate finalized membership terms must disclose price, renewal frequency, cancellation method, when cancellation takes effect, access after cancellation, refund handling, and material benefit changes. This draft authorizes no recurring charges, sets no refund exclusion, and creates no new purchase obligations. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

**Legal review required:** any liability limitation, exclusions, remedies, or caps. No blanket immunity, indemnity, or liability cap is established by this draft.

## 8. Termination and changes

A final agreement must specify material breach, notice, any reasonable opportunity to cure, termination consequences, and rights that survive termination. Membership cancellation must not be treated as software-license termination unless the finalized purchase and license terms clearly establish that result.

Material changes should be disclosed appropriately and must not retroactively remove existing license or consumer rights.

## 9. Third-party components and user-created content

Third-party notices and licenses must be preserved. Where they grant separate rights, those rights remain effective. Users retain rights they otherwise hold in their strategies or other original content; this draft transfers no such ownership to the project.
